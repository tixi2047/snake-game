How to play?



In the Snake game, the player uses the arrow keys to move the snake across the board. When the snake touches food, it eats it and grows by one segment. Regular (red) food is worth 1 point, while super (yellow) food is worth 10 points. The game ends when the snake hits the edge of the screen or collides with itself. The goal is to make the snake as long as possible before that happens.
